    How it works

    FairFare reads the fare, pickup and trip from the offer card. It takes off your running costs and gives a range, not a single number: the low end assumes slow traffic and some unpaid miles after drop-off, and the high end assumes a clean run. ACCEPT means even the low end hits your targets, DECLINE means even the high end misses them, and MARGINAL means it depends on the traffic.

    Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere. This demo reads pasted text; reading screenshots directly comes later. In the real app, the verdict shows only as one word and a colour while you're driving.

    Demo only. The numbers are estimates and haven't been checked against real drivers yet.
