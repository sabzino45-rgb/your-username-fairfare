- [ ] Product/Legal: decide whether FairFare may ever build a native Android app (MediaProjection screen capture or an Accessibility-service overlay) for live capture of the Uber Driver app. Owner and a solicitor decide, against Uber UK driver terms (ban on "scraping" and automated programs on the Driver App) and Google Play's Accessibility API policy. - [ ] Product/Legal: before share-to-FairFare (spec R1) ships to drivers, owner and solicitor confirm that a driver screenshotting one offer card themselves and having it read on their own phone is acceptable under Uber UK driver terms.

- [ ] Product/Safety: owner confirms the wording telling drivers that screenshot capture is for when parked only, and that offers arriving while driving are let go (taking a screenshot means handling the phone).

- [ ] Economics/Pricing: decide how FairFare would take payment (direct card payment on the web app vs. a store-listed app). economics.json assumes a 15% app-store fee; a web app on GitHub Pages pays card fees instead, which raises net revenue per user from about £3.53 to about £4.16 at the placeholder £4.99 before card fees. Owner decides; no price is changed.

Until then, all margin figures rest on a placeholder price with zero paying users.
