# Safety rule (human-owned: agents may not edit this file)

**No phone handling while driving.**

- The driver must never need to touch, type on or read detailed text on the phone while the vehicle is moving.
- Verdicts while moving: one glanceable word and colour (ACCEPT / MARGINAL / DECLINE), and optionally audio. No numbers to read, no buttons to press.
- FairFare never accepts or declines offers on the driver's behalf.
- Manual entry, settings and details are available only when the driver is parked.
- If the app can't tell whether the vehicle is moving, assume it is.

Changes need human sign-off and an entry in decisions.md.
